# Code of conduct

Alexandria is for everyone, and so is working on it.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Don't share anyone's private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to the people Alexandria is for.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and
may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem privately, use the contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
