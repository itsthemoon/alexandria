Alexandria · preview v0.1.0

An offline library for your computer.

Wikipedia, medical references, repair and survival guides, a world map, translation and a small AI that answers from those books. All on your computer. All working with no internet. No account, no tracking.

curl -fsSL https://itsthemoon.github.io/alexandria/install.sh | bash

Open Terminal, paste this, and press Return. Needs about 12 GB free. Read the script first.

What happens

  1. The app installs for you only — no admin password.
  2. You choose where the library goes: your home folder or an external drive.
  3. About 8 GB downloads, resuming if your connection drops.
  4. Every byte is checked against the release signature. If anything is wrong, it stops.
  5. Search is built on your computer (5 to 30 minutes). After that, it never goes online.

Good to know

  • It's a preview. The AI is small and can be wrong, so it always shows its sources.
  • The apps aren't signed with paid Apple or Microsoft certificates. The command above avoids the warning; the Releases page has normal downloads and the steps to open them.
  • Run the same command later to update. Only changed files download.
  • A Full edition with all of Wikipedia and street maps of the whole planet is next.

What it will not claim

  • That the content is true. A verified source can still be wrong.
  • That a computer with malware is safe. Malware can change what you see.
  • That a stolen signing key can't sign a bad release. That is why there are two keys and releases need approval.
